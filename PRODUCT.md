# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

International and domestic travelers comparing private rooms in Bhaktapur, often on mobile, who want a peaceful place near Durbar Square and Nyatapola Temple. They need to understand the rooms quickly, trust that the photographs are honest, find the property, and make a direct booking enquiry through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Present Holiday Home Bhaktapur as a welcoming, dependable local stay and turn interest into confident room and availability enquiries. Success means visitors can see the accommodation clearly, understand what is included, and reach the hosts without friction.

## Brand Personality

Peaceful, personal, authentically Bhaktapur. The voice is warm, plain-spoken, hospitable, and never grandiose.

## Anti-references

Anonymous international luxury hotels; generic booking templates; oversized copy that obscures the rooms; dominant glass cards; glowing text; pasted-on text highlights; decorative treatments that compete with photography; over-polished design that erases the feeling of a real local home.

## Design Principles

- Let real rooms and the Bhaktapur setting carry the first impression.
- Make hospitality feel personal through clarity and warmth, not luxury signals.
- Keep booking actions obvious without making the page feel transactional.
- Preserve honest, readable information across every language and device.
- Use local character selectively so the experience remains calm and contemporary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction standards. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, readable body text, and layouts that remain usable with longer German, French, and Spanish translations.
